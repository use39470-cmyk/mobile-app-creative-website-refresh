01 · The brief

An energy app that people read in two seconds.

Rise Science grew out of sleep coaching for pro teams like the Chicago Bulls, then launched the RISE app in 2021. It sells energy, not sleep scores: it models two levers, sleep debt and circadian rhythm, to show when you’ll peak and dip each day.

Design problems we chose

  1. 01

    Make invisible biology legible

    The energy curve is the product. It has to read in under two seconds, on the home screen, lock screen and wrist.

  2. 02

    Debt without guilt

    “Sleep debt” can feel like a scolding. Parents and shift-workers need it to motivate, not shame.

  3. 03

    From insight to action

    20+ timed habits is a lot. Turn them into one calm plan with a single “next best action”.

Our goal

One home screen that answers: how will I feel today, and what’s the one thing to do next?

02 · Research

Three people, three very different nights.

We built personas from Rise’s public audience (knowledge workers, parents and students), then stress-tested every screen against them.

Personas are Mobile App Creative research assumptions built from the brand’s public audience, not real customers.

05 · 3D wireframe

Take the home screen apart, layer by layer.

Every screen is built in five layers. Scroll and the exploded view assembles into the final UI. Drag to look around it.

Scroll to assemble · drag to orbit

  1. L1

    Layout grid

    4-column grid, 20pt margins, 8pt rhythm and safe areas for the island and home indicator.

  2. L2

    Wireframe

    Grey-box hierarchy: one hero number, the 24-hour curve, then the next three actions.

  3. L3

    Content & data

    Real copy and live data: energy now, peak countdown, sleep debt, the caffeine cut-off.

  4. L4

    Colour & type

    Night-sky indigo base, warm peach for energy peaks, violet for dips. Montserrat numbers.

  5. L5

    Motion & states

    The “now” marker breathes, peaks glow, and the curve redraws when last night’s sleep syncs.

Science · 2 min

Why your 3pm dip happens

Two systems drive your energy: sleep pressure, which builds every hour you’re awake, and your circadian rhythm, a roughly 24-hour clock. In the early afternoon the clock’s alerting signal briefly dips while pressure keeps rising, so you feel it.

The fix isn’t more coffee. Light, movement or a short nap before 3:30 pm lifts you without stealing tonight’s sleep.

Glanceable rules

  • One number. Time-to-next-peak beats any score.
  • Warm = energy, violet = rest. The colours carry meaning everywhere.
  • No red. Debt is shown as progress toward a target, never as an alarm.

09 · Measure

How we’d know it worked.

KPI 01

Time to first insight

We expect: New users see their first energy peak in under 90 seconds.

How we’d measure: Onboarding funnel timing, install → home screen.

KPI 02

Daily glances

We expect: Widget and watch views outnumber app opens 3:1.

How we’d measure: Widget timeline reloads vs. app sessions.

KPI 03

Habit adoption

We expect: Users with the caffeine cut-off on hit their Melatonin Window more often.

How we’d measure: Cohort comparison over 14 nights.

KPI 04

Week-4 retention

We expect: Guilt-free debt framing keeps parents and shift-workers engaged.

How we’d measure: Retention by persona segment, A/B on debt copy.

These are the success measures we would agree with the client before launch.
