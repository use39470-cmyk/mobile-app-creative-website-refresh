01 · Discover · The brief

A fitness app that says “rest” out loud.

Gentler Streak is an activity and health tracker for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, made by Gentler Stories, a small studio in Kranj, Slovenia, co-founded by Katarina Lotrič (CEO), Andrej Mihelič (UI/UX), Jasna Krmelj (CTO) and Luka Orešnik. Instead of fixed goals, it suggests how much to move each day based on readiness. Its orange, heart-shaped mascot, Yorhart, was created with illustrator Sören Selleslagh to stand for “what your heart would be telling you.” The line it lives by: “Move consistently, not constantly.”

Design problems we chose

  1. 01

    Readiness without anxiety

    Sleep, heart-rate variability and training load have to become one gentle sentence and one picture, not a score to worry about.

  2. 02

    Celebrate rest

    Streak mechanics usually reward volume. Here a rest day has to feel like part of the streak, because it is part of training.

  3. 03

    Systemic colour

    Each feature has its own colour, which a third-party teardown says can dilute recognition. We need a palette system anchored on the orange heart.

Our goal

One glance, on the phone or the wrist, that says how much to move today, and makes a rest day feel like a win.

02 · Define · Personas

Three people who were told to push harder.

Built from the audience Gentler Streak speaks to: Apple Watch owners put off by “push harder” fitness culture, including people returning from injury, burnout or illness, and people who track their cycle.

Personas are Mobile App Creative research assumptions built from the brand’s public audience, not real customers.

            06 · Design · 3D wireframe

            The Today screen, taken apart.

            Each screen is built in five layers, from grid to motion. Scroll and the exploded view assembles into the final UI; drag to look around it.

            Scroll to assemble · drag to orbit

            1. L1

              Layout grid

              4 columns, 20pt margins, an 8pt rhythm and safe areas. Every card spans the full grid so it scales with Dynamic Type.

            2. L2

              Wireframe

              One question per card, in reading order: how am I, where am I on my path, what should I do, what did I do.

            3. L3

              Content & data

              Plain-language copy first. “You’re steady” beats a score; the path shows ten days, with today ringed.

            4. L4

              Colour & type

              Warm cream base, Yorhart-orange for “today”, leaf green for the path. Heavy geometric numbers, sentence-case body.

            5. L5

              Motion & feedback

              The readiness orb breathes at a calm 5.5 s, today’s dot pulses once, and the band draws in left to right. All of it stops with Reduce Motion.

            11 · Ship · Measure

            How we’d know it worked.

            KPI 01

            Today, at a glance

            We expect: People can say what today’s suggestion is within five seconds of opening the app, or two seconds from the watch complication.

            How we’d measure: Moderated first-click and five-second tests; complication taps vs. app opens.

            KPI 02

            Rest feels like a win

            We expect: Testers describe the rest-day screen as encouraging, and people who take a suggested rest day come back the next day at least as often as those who skip it.

            How we’d measure: Interview coding across test rounds; next-day return rate by cohort after launch.

            KPI 03

            Findable features

            We expect: The four-tab IA lets people find key features first time in a tree test.

            How we’d measure: Tree test on the top eight tasks, then compared with the card-sort clusters.

            KPI 04

            Accessibility parity

            We expect: Task success with VoiceOver and the largest text sizes matches the default setup.

            How we’d measure: Sessions with assistive-technology users; audit at every release.

            These are the success measures we would agree with the client before launch.
