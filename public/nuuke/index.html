How we build

The flight plan.

From napkin to App Store in five stages. Every project follows the same countdown.

  1. T‑5

    Pre-flight · Discover

    Workshops, interviews and a teardown of the competition. We find the one problem worth solving first.

  2. T‑4

    Fuelling · Define

    Flows, information architecture and grey-box wireframes you can react to in days, not weeks.

  3. T‑3

    Ignition · Design

    Clickable prototypes, UI systems and motion. You tap through the product before a line of code exists.

  4. T‑2

    Lift-off · Build

    Native and cross-platform engineering, WebGL and 3D pipelines, tested on real devices.

  5. T‑1

    Orbit · Launch & grow

    Store launch, campaigns and analytics. We measure what changed and keep shipping.
