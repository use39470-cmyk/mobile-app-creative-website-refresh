03 · Values

How we think

Four principles we navigate by. Select each star to chart them.

Constellation · the Mobile App Creative n

01

Strategy before pixels

Every project starts with the problem worth solving, the people it’s for and the number that will prove it worked.

02

Show, don’t tell

You tap a prototype in week one and spin the 3D in week two. Decisions get made on real things, not slide decks.

03

One team, no hand-offs

Strategists, designers, engineers and marketers work in the same room, so what we design is what ships.

04

Built for longevity

Design systems, clean code and documentation, so your product keeps getting better after launch day.

04 · Mission log

Ways to work with us

Three mission profiles. Pick one to see how it flies.

Sub-orbital hop · 2–4 weeks

Validate an idea2–4 weeks

Sprint

Research, flows and a tappable prototype or 3D concept you can test with real users.

  1. Stage 01

    Kickoff

    Agree the problem worth solving, who it’s for and what would prove it worked.

  2. Stage 02

    Research & flows

    Map the journeys and the screens or scenes that matter most.

  3. Stage 03

    Prototype

    A tappable prototype or 3D concept, something real to react to.

  4. Stage 04

    Test & decide

    Put it in front of real users, then decide what to build next.

Ship a product2–6 months

Build

Design, engineering and launch of an app, 3D website or film, with a dedicated squad.

  1. Stage 01

    Squad & plan

    A dedicated squad, a scope and milestones everyone can see.

  2. Stage 02

    Design

    UI, design system and motion, built on what the research showed.

  3. Stage 03

    Engineer

    The app, 3D website or film, built in short cycles you can tap along the way.

  4. Stage 04

    Launch

    Ship it, document it and hand over a product that keeps improving.

Grow and iterateOngoing

Partner

Growth marketing, content and continuous product improvements on a monthly retainer.

  1. Stage 01

    Monthly plan

    Set the priorities for the month ahead together.

  2. Stage 02

    Content & campaigns

    Growth marketing and content that keeps the brand in motion.

  3. Stage 03

    Product improvements

    Continuous fixes and features, shipped as they’re ready.

  4. Stage 04

    Report & adjust
